Client support for the MCP Events extension (`io.modelcontextprotocol/events`, SEP-3415, Beta, protocol 2026-07-28+): `connection.supports("events")`, `client.listEvents(server?)` (cached under the SEP-2549 helpers, invalidated by `notifications/events/list_changed`), and `client.events(server, name, input, opts)` returning a subscribable (`subscribe` / `getSnapshot` / `close` / `refresh`) that polls `events/poll`, honours `nextPollMs` and `hasMore`, resumes from its last cursor across failures and reconnects, dedupes on `eventId`, and reports `truncated: true` as an explicit gap. Poll delivery only: `mode: "stream"` rejects with a clear `MCPError`, and webhook subscription is not driven. Built on mcp-query-defined schemas over the SDK's raw `request()` with a runtime probe for whether the installed SDK era-gates `events/*`, so it works on SDK 2.0.0. Every list and poll runs through the interceptor chain as a new `event` operation kind (`OperationKind` and `CallAuditEntry.kind` gain `"event"`; an interceptor that passes unknown kinds to `next` is unaffected, and a policy that denies unknown kinds will now deny events). New: `useEventTypes` / `useEvents` in `/react`; `verifyEventWebhook` / `generateWebhookSecret` in `/server` (Standard Webhooks receiver-side check); `MockMCPServer({ events })` with a controllable source in `/testing`.
